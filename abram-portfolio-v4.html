Selected work

Experimental engineering2026

Directional Active Noise Cancelling

Worked on a Raspberry Pi-based directional active noise-cancelling system. The project combined adaptive filtering, audio signal processing and hardware integration, with iterative laboratory testing and prototype development.

Python · DSP · FxLMS · Raspberry Pi · Experimental testing

The system achieved over 90% cancellation at 440 Hz in testing, with over 50% cancellation for multi-frequency signals after approximately one second.

Scientific data analysis2026

Measuring the Hubble Constant

Analysed galaxy spectra to estimate recession velocities and investigate the relationship between distance and velocity. Used Gaussian fitting to locate spectral lines, applied relativistic velocity calculations and performed weighted linear regression.

Python · NumPy · pandas · SciPy · Curve fitting

Obtained an estimate of approximately 70.3 ± 4.9 km s⁻¹ Mpc⁻¹ from the dataset.
