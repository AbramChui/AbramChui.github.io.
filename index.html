Worked on a Raspberry Pi-based directional active noise-cancelling system. The project combined adaptive filtering, audio signal processing and hardware integration, with iterative laboratory testing and prototype development.

Scientific data analysis2025

Measuring the Hubble Constant

Analysed galaxy spectra to estimate recession velocities and investigate the relationship between distance and velocity. Fitted Hβ spectral features using Gaussian profiles with linear backgrounds, propagated fitted-frequency uncertainties into velocity estimates, and compared the resulting velocities with galaxy distances.

Python · NumPy · pandas · SciPy · Matplotlib · Curve fitting
H₀ = 71.25 ± 4.98 km s⁻¹ Mpc⁻¹Weighted linear regression · 25 galaxies
View spectral fitting examples

Two representative examples: a relatively clean emission feature and a spectrum with a more structured, changing background.

View Hubble regression and uncertainty analysis

The fitted line centres were converted into recession velocities using the relativistic Doppler relation. The 25 retained galaxy measurements were compared with their distances using weighted linear regression, with weights based on the propagated spectral-fit velocity uncertainties.

Interpretation and limitations. The scatter of the galaxy measurements around the fitted line is much larger than the propagated spectral-fit uncertainties (reduced χ² ≈ 671). The quoted ±4.98 is the regression-parameter uncertainty after scaling by the observed residual scatter; it is not a complete uncertainty budget. Distance uncertainties and galaxy peculiar velocities have not yet been modelled.

03Established physics and active researchWell-developed scientific frameworks with questions still being investigated

Cosmology and the early Universe

Cosmic inflation, the cosmic microwave background, primordial nucleosynthesis, relic neutrinos, dark matter and dark energy. I am particularly interested in how observations from different cosmic epochs constrain the history and composition of the Universe.

Black-hole physics

Schwarzschild and Kerr solutions, black-hole thermodynamics, the no-hair theorem, event horizons, singularities in classical general relativity and proposed ways they might be resolved.

Compact objects and extreme astrophysics

Neutron stars, magnetars, possible quark stars and boson stars, accretion discs, relativistic jets and the extraction of rotational energy from black holes.

Black-hole formation and evolution

Stellar collapse, direct-collapse black-hole seeds, supermassive black-hole growth, galaxy mergers, gravitational waves and the final-parsec problem.

04Frontiers and speculative questionsOpen problems, hypothetical objects and ideas beyond established knowledge

Quantum gravity and unification

How gravity might be reconciled with quantum mechanics; quantum field theory, Yang–Mills theory, gauge symmetries, grand unification and the possibility of a more fundamental description of nature.

Unresolved questions about compact objects

Whether singularities are replaced by new physical structures, whether primordial black holes formed in the early Universe, and how hypothetical objects such as kugelblitze or exotic compact stars could fit within physical theory.

Advanced energy and future technologies

The limits of magnetic-confinement fusion, stellarators and tokamaks, Dyson swarms as hypothetical stellar-energy collectors, and self-replicating probes for interstellar exploration.

Spacetime engineering

The Alcubierre metric, warp-drive proposals, exotic spacetime geometries and the physical constraints on manipulating spacetime for faster-than-light effective travel.
